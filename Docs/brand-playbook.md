\#\# 1.2 Brand Name Treatment

\*\*Always written as:\*\* My Landlord Certificate (three words, title case)

\*\*Never written as:\*\*  
\- mylandlordcertificate (unless in a URL or handle)  
\- MY LANDLORD CERTIFICATE (only in logo lockup or specific design treatments)  
\- My Landlord Certificates (plural — that's the competitor; we are deliberately singular)  
\- M.L.C. or MLC (avoid acronyms in customer-facing copy; only acceptable in internal use)

\*\*The "My" matters.\*\* It's the distinctive element. Treat it with the same weight as the rest of the name — never drop it casually. "Book through Landlord Certificate" or "use Landlord Certificate" is wrong. Always "My Landlord Certificate."

\*\*Possessive use in marketing:\*\* Lean into the "my" framing where natural — "Your landlord certificate, sorted." "Make it your landlord certificate, not your headache." This reinforces the brand name inside copy.

\#\# 1.4 Logo Specifications

(Hand to your designer)

\*\*Required deliverables:\*\*  
\- Primary horizontal lockup: logo mark \+ wordmark side-by-side  
\- Stacked lockup: logo mark above wordmark  
\- Mark only: standalone for favicons, social profile pics, app icons  
\- Wordmark only: for footers and minimal contexts

\*\*Special consideration for this name:\*\* The brand has 3 words including a possessive ("My"). Designer needs to consider whether "My" gets visual emphasis (italicised, scripted, smaller) vs equal weight. Recommended: subtle differentiation — "My" slightly smaller or in italic, with "Landlord Certificate" as the visual anchor.

\*\*File formats required:\*\*  
\- AI (master)  
\- EPS (print)

\*\*Constraints:\*\*  
\- Must work in single black on white  
\- Must work in single white on Compliance Blue  
\- Must remain legible at 16x16px (favicon) — at this size, brief designer to focus on the mark, not the wordmark  
\- Minimum clear space: equal to the height of the "M" in "My" on all sides  
\- Never stretch, skew, rotate, or recolour outside approved palette

\#\# 1.7 Voice Principles

We sound like \*\*a knowledgeable, no-nonsense friend who happens to be qualified.\*\* Not a lawyer. Not a corporate vendor. Not a cheeky-chappie cowboy.

The "My" in our name signals that this is YOUR certificate — personal, owned, sorted. Voice should reinforce that ownership and friendliness without becoming overly familiar.

| We are | We are NOT |  
|---|---|  
| Clear | Vague |  
| Direct | Pushy |  
| Helpful | Patronising |  
| Confident | Arrogant |  
| Warm | Sycophantic |  
| Plain-spoken | Slangy |  
| Professional | Stiff |  
| Honest about cost | Misleading on price |

\#\# 1.8 Words: Use vs Avoid

\*\*Use:\*\*  
Your certificate · landlord · property owner · compliant · compliance · same-week · fast turnaround · accredited · NICEIC · Gas Safe · fixed price · no surprises · book now · sorted · simple · plain English

\*\*Avoid:\*\*  
World-class · best-in-class · leading provider · cowboy · rip-off · cheap · synergy · leverage · stakeholders · guys · chaps · "we hereby" · "kindly note"

\#\# 1.9 Voice Examples (Same Message, Three Ways)

\*\*The message:\*\* Reminding a landlord their EICR expires in 30 days.

❌ \*\*Too corporate:\*\*  
"Dear valued customer, we wish to advise that your Electrical Installation Condition Report is approaching its expiration date. Please contact our customer service team to schedule a renewal at your earliest convenience."

❌ \*\*Too casual:\*\*  
"Hey\! 👋 Your EICR is about to run out lol — book it in mate before you get a fine 😬"

✅ \*\*Just right:\*\*  
"Hi Sarah — your EICR expires on 12 June. Renewing takes 5 minutes online and we can have an engineer out next week. Avoid the rush (and the £30k non-compliance fine): book your renewal here."

\# PART 2: CONTENT STRATEGY

\#\# 2.1 Strategic Frame

Organic content has three jobs at My Landlord Certificate:

1\. \*\*Build trust for paid traffic\*\* — when ad clickers check us, our profiles must look credible  
2\. \*\*Generate organic discovery\*\* — landlords searching, scrolling, asking questions can find us without ads  
3\. \*\*Build community \+ retention\*\* — keep past customers engaged for renewals

We treat these as equal goals. Organic IS a growth channel here, alongside paid.

\*\*Critical SEO note:\*\* Because the brand name "My Landlord Certificate" is itself a high-volume keyword cluster, organic content has an outsized SEO benefit here. Every blog post, social post, and YouTube video that uses the brand name reinforces our claim on the keyword. This compounds. Be intentional about it.

\#\# 2.2 The 4-Pillar Content Framework

Every piece of content fits one of four pillars. Aim for this weekly mix:

| Pillar | Weekly % | Purpose |  
|---|---|---|  
| \*\*Education\*\* | 40% | Build authority and answer real questions landlords/agents are asking |  
| \*\*Trust & Behind-the-Scenes\*\* | 25% | Real engineers, real jobs, real proof we deliver |  
| \*\*Service & Promotional\*\* | 20% | Specific services, prices, offers — drive bookings |  
| \*\*Community & Engagement\*\* | 15% | Polls, questions, conversations that build audience |

\#\# 2.3 Content Pillar Deep-Dive

\#\#\# Pillar 1: Education (40%)

\*\*Audience:\*\* Both landlords AND estate agents — but estate agents need a more technical/regulatory tone.

\*\*B2C landlord topics:\*\*  
\- "What is an EICR?" plain-English explainers  
\- Renewal timelines for each certificate type  
\- Cost guides ("how much should an EICR cost in 2026?")  
\- What happens if you fail/miss a certificate  
\- Specific scenarios ("just inherited a flat, what do I need?")  
\- Renters' Rights Act 2026 changes  
\- Common myths busted

\*\*B2B estate agent topics:\*\*  
\- Compliance process improvements  
\- Bulk certificate management  
\- Risk management for property portfolios  
\- Letting agent legal obligations  
\- New regulation summaries (Renters' Rights Act, MEES)

\#\#\# Pillar 2: Trust & Behind-the-Scenes (25%)

\*\*Topics:\*\*  
\- "Meet the team" — engineer profiles  
\- Day-in-the-life of an engineer  
\- Office / van / equipment shots  
\- Real customer testimonials with names \+ areas  
\- Trustpilot review screenshots  
\- Before/after job photos (with permission)  
\- "Today's job" updates

\#\#\# Pillar 3: Service & Promotional (20%)

\*\*Topics:\*\*  
\- Service spotlights with fixed pricing  
\- Bundle deals (EICR \+ Gas Safety packages)  
\- "Limited slots this week" availability posts  
\- Seasonal angles ("New tenancy starting? Here's your checklist")  
\- Estate agent referral programme  
\- Multi-property discounts

\#\#\# Pillar 4: Community & Engagement (15%)

\*\*Topics:\*\*  
\- Polls ("What's your biggest compliance headache?")  
\- Open questions ("Worst landlord story?")  
\- Tag-a-friend posts  
\- Local hooks ("If you're in Croydon, we have next-week slots")  
\- Industry news commentary

\#\# 2.4 Audience Split in Content (75/25)

Roughly 75% of content should speak primarily to private landlords. 25% should speak primarily to small/medium estate agents.

\*\*How to signal which audience a post is for:\*\*

\- B2C language: "your property," "your tenant," "your renewal"  
\- B2B language: "your portfolio," "your clients' properties," "your team's compliance workload"  
\- Use channel as a signal: Instagram skews B2C; LinkedIn skews B2B; Facebook is mixed

\*\*Don't try to please both in one post.\*\* Pick the audience for that piece and write directly to them. Mixed-audience content always feels watered down.

\#\# 2.5 Channel Strategy by Audience

| Channel | Audience priority | Posting cadence | Time investment |  
|---|---|---|---|  
| \*\*Facebook\*\* | 70% B2C, 30% B2B | 4–5 posts/week | 4–5 hrs/week |  
| \*\*Instagram\*\* | 90% B2C, 10% B2B | 4 posts/week \+ 2 Reels \+ daily Stories | 5–6 hrs/week |  
| \*\*LinkedIn\*\* | 90% B2B, 10% B2C | 2–3 posts/week (founder \+ page) | 2–3 hrs/week |

Total weekly time investment: \*\*11–14 hours.\*\*

\#\# 2.6 Content Production Workflow

To make 11–14 hrs/week sustainable, batch production:

\*\*Day 1 (Monday, 3 hrs):\*\* Plan & write  
\- Outline next 7 days of posts across all channels  
\- Write all captions  
\- Draft DM/comment response templates

\*\*Day 2 (Tuesday, 3 hrs):\*\* Visual production  
\- Create graphics in Canva (templates speed this up massively)  
\- Edit any photos  
\- Source/create stock-style B-roll

\*\*Day 3 (Wednesday/Thursday, 3 hrs):\*\* Reels / video  
\- Batch-shoot 4 Reels in one session every 2 weeks  
\- Edit in CapCut

\*\*Day 4 (Friday, 2 hrs):\*\* Schedule \+ engage  
\- Schedule everything in Buffer/Later for the week  
\- 60 min on community management (replies, comments)

\#\# 2.7 The Editorial Calendar

Use this as a default weekly rhythm. Customise as you find what works.
