> **Quick answer:** Averaged over each certificate's lifespan, a typical London **1–3 bedroom single let with a gas boiler** costs about **£180 a year** to keep compliant, including the new **£65 PRS Database fee**. A **five-bedroom furnished HMO** costs roughly **£900 a year** before the council's HMO licence fee.

Most landlords think about compliance one certificate at a time, usually when a renewal reminder lands. That makes costs feel unpredictable. Averaging each certificate across its lifespan gives a steady annual figure you can plan for.

This guide uses our fixed London prices. Other providers will differ, but the structure of the budget is the same everywhere in England.

## What changed for 2026–27

Two new costs appear in 2026–27 budgets.

**The PRS Database fee.** The government confirmed on 9 September 2026 that the "Register your rental property" service will roll out region by region from 15 December 2026. The draft regulations set a fee of **£65 per property**, renewed annually. For a five-property portfolio, that is £325 a year.

**The landlord ombudsman.** Membership will become mandatory under the Renters' Rights Act 2025. The fee has not yet been announced, so it is left out of the figures below.

A few extras may apply. Smoke and carbon monoxide alarms cost little but must be working at the start of every tenancy. If you provide appliances, a PAT test from **£59.99** is good practice. If the property is in the Congestion Charge Zone, add **£20** per visit, and **£10** if there is no free parking.

The council HMO licence fee is on top. It varies widely between London boroughs, so check your council's current fee schedule.

## Three ways to bring the cost down

**Align renewal dates.** Gas safety records can be renewed up to two months early without losing your anniversary date. Use that window to bring certificates into one visit.

**Book as a bundle.** When an EICR, gas safety certificate, fire risk assessment and fire safety certificate are booked together, our HMO bundle applies **10% off** automatically. Our [landlord certificate bundles](/landlord-certificates-bundle) also start at **£160** for EICR plus gas safety with a boiler service.

**Fix issues before the visit.** Test smoke alarms, clear access to the consumer unit and boiler, and make sure every room is accessible. A failed first visit is the most common hidden cost.

## What non-compliance costs instead

Compare those budgets with the penalties. The maximum civil penalty for breaching the electrical safety rules is **£40,000 per breach**. Failing to maintain a gas safety record is a criminal offence. For unlicensed HMOs, rent repayment orders can now reach **24 months** of rent. Compliance is one of the cheapest lines in a landlord's budget.
